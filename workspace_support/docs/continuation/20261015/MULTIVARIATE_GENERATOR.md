# Source-conditioned multivariate generator

Root decision, 2026-10-15, incorporating the user's continuing requirements.

The target is a generator of plausible daily and archaeological objects with jointly
controlled form, meaningful decoration, material and process. It is not a set of
independent arbitrary shape sliders, source labels, or uniformly colored test solids.

## Construction policy

Use an existing suitable open model, adapt a model with documented deficiencies,
or construct the missing geometry when existing resources cannot meet the task.
All imported resources receive a refinement review, including resources already
of good quality. Preserve the original and license; produce a separate derivative
with explicit edits and evidence. Refinement must preserve meaningful rim, foot,
handle, spout, inscription and forming features. Software closure does not establish
that a scan captured a physical inner wall or bottom.

The recipe separates `family`, `style`, `source_anchor`, controls, material,
decoration and process. A style anchor includes actual geometry or a profile,
not only a catalogue ID. Per-region coverage distinguishes observed geometry,
reported dimensions, interpreted features, modeled additions and unknowns.

Common controls include dimensions and physical scale. Dedicated controls express
the selected family: bowl depth/belly/rim/foot; jar belly width/height, shoulders,
neck and mouth; handled vessels additionally have handle/spout/attachment controls.
Categorical construction branches are selected before continuous variation.
Parameter domains and dependencies are source-supported where evidence exists and
explicit design exploration otherwise. A few objects do not establish a population
distribution. Explicit user controls are not silently repaired or replaced.

## Form, decoration and material remain coupled

Decoration is a content and layout recipe, including subjects, their relationships,
main scene, borders, blank space, application process and placement on actual
surface regions. Known curatorial meaning is retained; unknown meaning is not
invented. Vector paths may represent brush deposition, but a generic icon is not
evidence of an authentic vessel motif. Reuse and refine open motif resources with
the same attribution and modification record as geometry.

Material includes body, coating, local variation and surface appearance. Original
glazed interior surfaces remain glazed where the reference supports this. New
fracture surfaces expose body material. Appearance layers do not become mechanical
glaze solids unless they are actually constructed and meshed. Optical assumptions
and mechanical measurements have separate status. Metals, wood, glass and ceramics
cannot silently share one brittle ceramic law.

Paint and material fields are frozen in mother/rest coordinates. An incision or
relief that affects mechanics changes the mother before volume meshing. The same
actual geometry identity must reach meshing, native fracture and observation;
render-only geometry must not silently replace it.

## Initial reusable backends and evidence

- Existing A12 24 registered parameter models and 16 pattern families remain
  execution backends. Their broad defaults are controls until source reviewed.
- VF smooth meridian CAD supports exact source-edge face roles and explicit
  rounded wall/rim/base/foot construction. Daily and historical footed bowl anchors
  and independent design variants now exist; section assumptions remain visible.
- Apsida SHM 17946:0047 provides a real open double-handled footed bowl mesh.
  The raw asset is retained; its scale is an explicit catalogue-derived hypothesis.
  Separate refinement derivatives removed islands and exact zero-area/coincident
  triangles. Seven pinched vertex links remain: the current derivative is a visual
  reference, not a qualified native solid for simulation.
- Generic STEP meshing now works on a full-size bowl diagnostic. Exterior face
  labels did not survive Gmsh import. Individually exported native face witnesses
  and two-stage OCC Boolean history now propagate all 16 source identities through
  the actual v6 CAD cut under declared tolerances. Production-mesh integration
  and geometric accuracy are being reviewed separately.
- Reference material/pattern adapters and a recipe-controlled composition path
  now produce source-guided Tokusa and plain Jun variants on actual CAD mothers.
  A meaningful photo-derived partial dragon field has metric pigment spreading
  on the actual bowl curve; its unified generation route and display are under
  review. Partial fired-photo projection is intermediate reference material,
  not a finished refined ornament or a historical bowl reconstruction.

The next acceptance is an actual side/top/bottom/section variant matrix followed
by the same mothers decorated and shaded, with recipes that reproduce the arrays.
Then one refined mother must traverse native fracture and A12 observation. This
document does not establish that all families, motifs, materials or physics are
qualified; the original overall goal stays open.
