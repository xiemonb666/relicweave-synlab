# RelicWeave V1.47 A6.14/A6.24 primary-source audit

**Scope.** I read the 16 entries in A6.24 and the paper-to-UA-IFRMN mappings in document table 25, plus A6.19/A6.22 where the claims recur. I checked titles, current version/venue notes, task, and method against author-hosted preprints, official proceedings, or publisher pages. Descriptions below are paraphrases; no long paper excerpts are reproduced.

**Overall finding.** The sources exist and mostly support the narrow method descriptions in table 25. The proposed application is still an *analogy/transfer hypothesis*: these papers do not establish UA-IFRMN, typed R0/R1/R2 inference, vessel identity recovery, or performance on real archaeological sherds. Do not present their results as proof of UA-IFRMN novelty, real-world vessel physics, or RelicWeave performance. Keep each source's original task and evidence boundary explicit.

**Corrections worth applying before treating A6.24 as current:**

- SARe's cited *Large-Scale* title and the fracture-token/contact-graph description are the v1 (23 March 2026) state. The latest checked arXiv v2 (1 August 2026) is titled *Structure-Aware Generative 3D Fragment Reassembly* and describes query-aligned local geometry and task-native structural supervision integrated with point flow, plus inference-time geometric verification and resampling. Cite a version deliberately; do not mix title/mechanism across versions.
- The GARF table's “one-step preassembly” is the earlier arXiv wording. The ICCV 2025 proceedings paper describes **two-session flow matching** at inference. If the plan cites ICCV 2025, align the mechanism description to the proceedings version.
- TORA's arXiv v2 says it was accepted to **ECCV 2026**; MV-RoMa's arXiv entry says **CVPR 2026 accepted**. The arXiv-only bibliography is valid as a preprint record but incomplete as a current venue note.
- Coconut was indeed listed/presented as an ICLR 2025 workshop paper, but the current arXiv v4 (23 August 2026) and COLM 2025 accepted-paper list record it as **accepted to COLM 2025**. Prefer COLM 2025 as the conference citation and mention the workshop only if that history matters.
- The “ICCV Highlight” for Combinative Matching and “NeurIPS Spotlight” for Rectified Point Flow are supported by author/project pages; the official proceedings confirm the respective ICCV and NeurIPS main-conference venues.

## UA-IFRMN claim boundary

Primary-source verification supports these as *prior-art building blocks*: local geometric/contact evidence and refinement (SARe, GARF, RPF, Combinative Matching); same-source retrieval and iterative graph expansion (Bag of Bags, MHopReg); multiview inconsistency as a caution for chained pairwise decisions (MV-RoMa); structural feature distillation (TORA); completion priors/missing tokens (Jigsaw++, Beyond Reassembly); and persistent/short-long/adaptive memories (Titans, VisMem, WorldMM, Coconut).

The **combination and interpretation remain UA-IFRMN hypotheses**: a posterior over R0/R1/R2/UNKNOWN, explicit separation of `same-vessel` from direct contact, provenance-preserving soft writes, reversible revision, and contact-seeded expansion to retrieve nonadjacent members. None of these 16 sources reports that exact model or validates it on RelicWeave's target data. A6's “candidate/idea” and go/no-go evaluation language is therefore important and should remain attached to the name.
