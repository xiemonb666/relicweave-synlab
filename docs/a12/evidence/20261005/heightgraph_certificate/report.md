## Finding

Following root's Astra review, those paths remain untouched: the evidence identifies the defect, but does not authorize a Boolean bypass.

P02 calls `NB.edge_recession_body`, checks its closed volume, then intersects that body with the source ([physical_narrowband.py:182](synlab/degrade/physical_narrowband.py)). The operation is intended as a material-subset guard, but in these cases its degenerate seam output is less valid than the already-built body.

## Root decision and isolated source candidate

Root adopted the limited exact height-graph certifier as a diagnostic candidate, and rejected the generic Boolean bypass, vertex movement, and hole repair. The only new implementation is `synlab/degrade/heightgraph_certificate.py`, with focused contract tests in `tests/a12/degrade/test_heightgraph_certificate_20261005.py`; review notes and the post-review test command are in `source-review-plan.md`. It is not imported by production code and does not modify `physical_narrowband.py`, `sdf.py`, `api.py`, axes, G01 files, or stored failure data.

The candidate first validates the source's upper/lower patch coverage, strict positive thickness, complete side curtains, and closed topology; only then does it load and assess candidate relationships or quality that can return `FAIL`. Mesh inputs must be genuine NumPy arrays (ndarray subclasses normalize to a base view) with verified rank-2 `N×3` shape, dtype, logical bytes, and at most 5,000 vertices/10,000 faces before `.tolist()`. Domain and quality rational/integer inputs are limited to 2,048 numerator/denominator bits, and the pair counter has a hard 250,000 ceiling. These limit input sizes and bbox candidate-pair count only; they do not formally bound intermediate Fraction growth or total runtime. The exact Fraction overlay, coordinate contract, and normalized geometry-array semantic SHA remain diagnostic only and do not authenticate source provenance.

The twelve contract tests include the original ten cases, the source-invalid-before-candidate counterexample (`source` center `-3`, bottom `-2`, candidate flat at `0` must yield `UNKNOWN`), and bounded-input checks for pair ceiling, integer/Fraction bits, and role tuple size. A deceptive array-like is rejected, and an ndarray subclass with false shape/dtype/nbytes metadata must be judged from its normalized base view. Their quality thresholds are fixture-only, not a qualification of actual geometry. The root-authorized 12-case contract run passed, but no frozen P02/P03 candidate array has been tested, so qualification remains **UNKNOWN**. The method is an engineering candidate, not an established novelty claim. P03 wall guard, physical QA, captured-noise observations, and detailed labels stay UNKNOWN/blocked.

The independent static review also prompted a bounded-input clarification: array element counts and bounding-box candidate-pair counts are limited, but this source does not establish a formal worst-case bound for intermediate Fraction bit growth or whole-call runtime. ## P03 wall QA and label boundary

The geometry finalization defect does not qualify P03's physical wall guard. `_erode_interface` currently runs `NB.paired_thickness` only as a diagnostic and explicitly records `wall_guard.status="UNKNOWN"` ([physical_narrowband.py:334](synlab/degrade/physical_narrowband.py), [physical_narrowband.py:379](synlab/degrade/physical_narrowband.py)). `_assemble` accepts a P03 result only when the guard is absent or literal `PASS`; `UNKNOWN` leaves physical acceptance false and suppresses physical truth ([physical_narrowband.py:98](synlab/degrade/physical_narrowband.py)). That fail-closed boundary should remain.

The legacy probe cannot be promoted to the 0.6 mm guard: it offsets each source sample by `+face_normal * eps` and then takes the first hit along `-normal` ([narrowband.py:368](synlab/degrade/narrowband.py)). On the recorded rough probe, it starts outside the wall, hits the source wall at the first entry, has only 164/400 paired rays, and the pre-clip minimum thickness is about 0.002 mm. The computed violation fraction excludes unpaired rays and only counts a relative loss above 50%; it does not enforce an absolute 0.6 mm minimum. `thickness_guard_satisfied=1.0` in the old measured dictionary is therefore not physical wall evidence. The new `wall_intervals` helper is explicitly independent of P03 and needs trusted wall stations/directions and material-subset evidence; its 16 local tests did not connect it to P03. Interface face normals alone do not identify the vessel wall direction ([wall_intervals.py:1](synlab/degrade/wall_intervals.py), [wall_intervals.py:55](synlab/degrade/wall_intervals.py)).

The eventual target—true geometry erosion, captured observation noise, and detailed labels—therefore needs at least three separately evidenced outcomes: valid delivered geometry, qualified P03 wall/physical QA, and observation plus label recomputation tied to the accepted geometry hash. `Measurement.valid` records that a measurement record exists; it is not physical QA. Current failure evidence has valid measurement records alongside `postassembly_status=FAIL`, and label dependencies remain invalidated/pending. No capture or label may be promoted by the candidate topology repair alone.
