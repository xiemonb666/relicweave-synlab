MODULE DOC: Physically driven fracture-surface morphology on one shared partition plane.

Why this module exists
----------------------
``plane_shared_cut`` produces a mathematically perfect plane: correct, but it carries no
fracture *morphology*, and fracture morphology is the strongest signal a neighbour-piece
retrieval stage can read off a sherd edge.  ``rough_shared_cut`` adds a frozen finite-Fourier
height field, but its coefficients are drawn from the RNG rather than derived from the body, so
the relief it produces cannot be tied to the material or to the wall it broke out of.

MODULE DOC: Measurement of a realized fracture surface: roughness, correlation length, ridge density.

Everything here measures the **realized discrete geometry** (the cutter top surface that both
sides of the event consumed, and the interface faces the boolean actually produced), never the
analytic field the model asked for.  That distinction is the point: the parameters in
:mod:`synlab.fracture.morphology` are the *request*, these numbers are the *realization*, and the
report publishes both.
